# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

Welcome to my first repository! Here is a brief overview of one of my favorite sci-fi series.

---

*Stranger Things* is an American science fiction horror drama television series created by the **Duffer Brothers**. Set in the 1980s in the fictional town of *Hawkins, Indiana*, the plot centers around the disappearance of a young boy and the supernatural events occurring around the town.

> "Friends don't lie." — **Eleven**

---

* **Genre:** Sci-Fi, Horror, Drama, Mystery
* **Setting:** 1980s Nostalgia & Synth Soundtrack
* **Main Themes:**
  1. Friendship and loyalty
  2. Secret government experiments
  3. Parallel dimensions (*The Upside Down*)

---
